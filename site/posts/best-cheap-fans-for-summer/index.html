What to Look For

Before you add a fan to your cart, keep these five things in mind:

  • Airflow (CFM): Look for a fan with at least 1,500 CFM (cubic feet per minute) for a large room. Under 1,000 CFM is fine for a desk or bedside table.
  • Noise Level: Check the decibel rating if listed. Under 50 dB is quiet enough for sleeping. Anything above 60 dB will be noticeable during a movie or conversation.
  • Oscillation: A fan that swivels side to side cools a much wider area than one that blows straight. Don't skip this feature on a room fan.
  • Size and Footprint: Tower fans take up less floor space but often push less air. Pedestal fans are taller and move more air but need more room.
  • Controls and Remote: A remote is worth a few extra dollars so you don't have to get up to change settings. Digital touch controls are nice, but simple dials are more reliable long-term.
