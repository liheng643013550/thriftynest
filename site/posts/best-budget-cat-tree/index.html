Best Budget Cat Trees (Tall, Sturdy & Under $60)

2026-08-20 · Pets

Cats love to climb. Your furniture, unfortunately, does not. If you’re looking for a way to give your feline friend a high perch without spending a small fortune, you need a solid budget cat tree. The good news is that you don’t have to spend $150 to get something sturdy and tall. Here are the best budget cat trees under $60 that actually hold up to daily use.

What to Look For

Before you click "buy," keep these five criteria in mind. They separate the junk from the genuinely good deals.

  • Stability and Base Width: A tall tree is useless if it tips over. Look for a base that is at least as wide as the tallest post is long, or check reviews specifically for "wobbly." A heavy, dense base is your best friend.
  • Post Thickness: The scratching posts are the skeleton of the tree. Cheap trees often use 2-inch diameter posts, which bend and break. Look for posts that are at least 3 to 4 inches in diameter for durability.
  • Material Quality: Avoid trees covered in that thin, scratchy "fur" that sheds everywhere. Look for plush, dense faux fur or carpet covering. Also, check that the particleboard platforms are at least 0.5 inches thick.
  • Height vs. Ceiling: Measure your ceiling height. While most trees are around 40 to 60 inches tall, you want to make sure the top perch is high enough to satisfy your cat but not so tall that it hits your ceiling fan.
  • Assembly Hardware: The biggest failure point on budget trees is the hardware. Look for metal brackets and bolts (not plastic) that connect the posts to the platforms. Also, make sure an Allen wrench is included in the box.
