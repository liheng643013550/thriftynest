What to Look For

Before you hit “buy,” keep these five criteria in mind. They are the difference between a handy tool and a paperweight.

  • Suction Power (Pa): Look for at least 2000Pa. Anything less struggles with area rugs and pet hair. More is better, but over 3000Pa is rare at this price.
  • Navigation: Avoid models that rely purely on random bump-and-turn navigation if you have an open floor plan. Look for gyroscope or LiDAR navigation for systematic cleaning patterns. It saves time and battery.
  • Battery Life: A 150-minute battery is the sweet spot. It allows the vacuum to finish a standard two-bedroom apartment in one go. Anything under 90 minutes is frustrating.
  • Brushroll Type: If you have pets or long hair, a brushless rubber roller (like those from newer models) is a lifesaver. They don’t tangle. If you have only hard floors, a standard bristle brush is fine.
  • App Connectivity: You want a model that connects to Wi-Fi and works with Alexa or Google Home. Also, check if the app allows virtual boundaries (no-go zones). This is a premium feature that is starting to trickle down to budget models.

eufy RoboVac 11S MAX

Price: ~$130

The eufy 11S MAX is a legend in the budget space for one simple reason: it is incredibly thin. At just 2.85 inches tall, it slides under most sofas, bed frames, and low cabinets that other vacuums get stuck on. The suction is rated at 2000Pa, which is plenty for hard floors and low-pile rugs.

What makes it great is the "Max" mode. You can toggle it on for a deeper clean, but it also has a standard mode that runs very quietly. The drop-proof sensors and bump navigation are basic, but they work. It also comes with a remote control, which is handy if you don't want to fiddle with a phone app.

Downsides: The navigation is random. It bounces around until the battery dies, so it might miss spots or take a long time to cover a large room. There is no mapping feature, and the dustbin is on the smaller side, so you will need to empty it frequently, especially if you have pets.

Who should buy it: If you have a smaller home with mostly hard floors and low furniture, this is the best value for the money. The slim profile is a game-changer that you won't find on many other models at this price point.

ILIFE V3s Pro

Price: ~$99

The ILIFE V3s Pro is the budget hero for pet owners. It features a tangle-free brushroll that is designed specifically to resist hair wrap. If you have shedding pets, this is the first thing you should check in the mornings, and this vacuum handles it better than most.

This is a no-frills machine. It uses a remote control and has a simple bump navigation system. It doesn't connect to Wi-Fi or have an app. However, it is very good at what it does: picking up dirt, dust, and pet hair on hard floors and low carpets. The suction is decent, and it has a large 600ml dustbin, which means less frequent emptying.

Downsides: The lack of a smart app is the biggest drawback for some. You cannot schedule cleanings from your phone or monitor its progress. The bump navigation is also inefficient, so it may run for a long time before covering all areas.

Who should buy it: This is for the person who wants a cheap, reliable helper that they can set with a remote and let it run. If you don't care about smart home features and just want to stop sweeping up pet hair every day, this is a great pick.

Wyze Robot Vacuum

Price: ~$149

The Wyze Robot Vacuum is the disruptor in this category. It brings LiDAR navigation—a feature usually found in $400+ vacuums—down to under $150. This means it actually maps your home in real-time and cleans in neat, efficient rows rather than bouncing around randomly.

The app is surprisingly robust. You can see the map as it cleans, set no-go zones (virtual walls) to keep it away from cords or pet bowls, and target specific rooms. The suction power is rated at 1100Pa, which is lower than the eufy, but the cleaning pattern is so much more efficient that it makes up for it on hard floors.

Downsides: The suction is weaker than others on this list. It can struggle with high-pile carpets and thick area rugs. The dustbin is also a bit small, and the filter needs frequent cleaning to maintain airflow.

Who should buy it: If you have a home with multiple rooms and want the vacuum to work smart, not hard, this is the one. The mapping feature is a premium experience that you will appreciate daily, as long as you have mostly hard floors.

Lefant M210

Price: ~$120

The Lefant M210 is a slim, lightweight option that is perfect for smaller spaces. It shares a similar profile to the eufy, making it great for getting under furniture. It offers 2000Pa of suction, which is solid for this price.

It also has a few modern touches, like the ability to control it via a remote or the Lefant app. The app is basic, but it allows you to schedule cleanings and switch between power modes. It has a 150-minute runtime, which is excellent for a small apartment. It also has anti-drop sensors and can climb over thresholds up to 0.6 inches.

Downsides: The app is not as polished as Wyze or eufy's. Some users report connectivity issues. The cleaning path is still random, so it can be repetitive in open spaces.

Who should buy it: This is a great choice for a studio apartment or a single bedroom. It is cheap, quiet, and does a great job on hard floors daily. If you need something to keep the kitchen clean between deeper cleans, this fits the bill.

Anker Eufy RoboVac G30

Price: ~$149

The RoboVac G30 is a step up from the 11S MAX. It introduces gyroscope navigation, which helps the vacuum clean in a more organized, zig-zag pattern rather than completely random bouncing. It also boasts 2000Pa of suction and a feature called BoostIQ, which automatically increases suction power when it detects carpet.

The app is also better here. You can schedule cleaning, adjust suction levels, and see a basic cleaning history. It works with Alexa and Google Assistant for voice control. The dustbin has a HEPA filter, which is a nice bonus for allergy sufferers.

Downsides: It is taller than the 11S MAX, so it might not fit under very low furniture. The gyroscope navigation is better than random, but it still doesn't map your home like the Wyze. It can get confused in open spaces.

Who should buy it: If your home has a mix of hard floors and low-pile carpets, this is the best all-rounder. The gyroscope navigation provides a good balance of efficiency and price, and the BoostIQ feature is genuinely useful.

iRobot Roomba 692

Price: ~$149

You can't talk about robot vacuums without mentioning iRobot. The Roomba 692 is an older model, but it is often found at this price point. It is a reliable workhorse with a proven track record. It uses iRobot's patented Dirt Detect sensors to focus on areas that are extra dirty.

The app is one of the best in the budget category. It is user-friendly and allows for scheduling. It has strong suction and works exceptionally well on carpets, which is where iRobot excels. It also has a dual multi-surface rubber brushes, which are good for picking up debris without getting tangled.

Downsides: The navigation is random, and it can be noisy compared to the eufy. The battery life is about 90 minutes, which is lower than the competition. It also lacks a boundary system unless you buy separate virtual wall barriers.

Who should buy it: If you trust the iRobot brand and want a vacuum that handles carpet well, this is a safe bet. It is not the most high-tech, but it is reliable and backed by a major brand.

The Bottom Line

So, are robot vacuums under $150 worth it? Yes, absolutely, but with a caveat. They are not going to replace your full-size vacuum for deep cleaning, and they won't give you the hands-free experience of a $700 model that empties itself and maps every room.

However, they are perfect for daily maintenance. Running one of these every day will keep your floors feeling clean and reduce the amount of dirt tracked through your home. The key is to match the vacuum to your home.

If you have a small apartment with hard floors, grab the Lefant M210 or the eufy RoboVac 11S MAX. If you want smart navigation, the Wyze Robot Vacuum is the clear winner. And if you have pets and a tight budget, the ILIFE V3s Pro is a no-brainer.

For most people, we recommend the Anker Eufy RoboVac G30. It offers the best balance of price, features, and performance for a typical home. Skip the expensive models, grab one of these, and enjoy having slightly cleaner floors without lifting a finger.
