The 7 Best Air Fryers Under $50 in 2026 (Tested & Compared)

2026-08-18 · Kitchen & Cooking

Air fryers changed how a lot of us cook, and the good news is you no longer need to spend $150 to get one that actually works. After weeks of testing budget models at home, these are the air fryers under $50 we would actually buy — and the ones we'd skip.

What to look for in a budget air fryer

  • Capacity: 2 to 3.5 quarts is the sweet spot for 1-3 people. Bigger sounds better but takes longer to preheat and uses more counter space.
  • Wattage: Look for 1400W or more. Lower wattage models cook slowly and turn out soggy fries.
  • Dishwasher-safe basket: You will use this thing daily. A nonstick, dishwasher-safe basket is worth more than any extra preset button.
  • Noise: Budget models range from "white noise" to "hair dryer." Check reviews for noise complaints.
  • Presets: Nice to have, but a manual temperature dial is honestly more reliable at this price.

Chefman TurboFry 3.6QT

The TurboFry is our overall pick for most households. The 3.6-quart basket fits a full meal for two, it's dishwasher-safe, and at roughly $50 it sits right at the edge of our budget. Cooking is even enough that we rarely need to shake the basket mid-cycle.

Downsides: It runs a little louder than premium models, and the dial controls feel basic.

Who should buy it: Anyone who cooks for two or three and wants a no-fuss daily workhorse. Chefman TurboFry 3.6QT

Cosori Mini 2.1QT

Cosori is known for making some of the best air fryers at any price, and their mini model is the best small air fryer under $50. The 2.1-quart basket is perfect for one person — a single serving of fries or a chicken breast fits easily. It preheats fast and cooks more evenly than most models twice its size.

Downsides: Too small for a family. No presets on the base model.

Who should buy it: Singles, students, and anyone with a tiny counter. Cosori Mini 2.1QT

Bella Pro Series 2.6QT

The Bella Pro is the cheapest air fryer we'd recommend without hesitation. At around $30 it's often on sale for less. It does the basics — fries, nuggets, roasted vegetables — and does them well enough. The controls are two dials: temperature and time. That's it. Nothing to break.

Downsides: Small capacity, and it cooks slightly less evenly than the Chefman or Cosori.

Who should buy it: First-time air fryer buyers who aren't sure they'll use one daily. Bella Pro Series 2.6QT

Crux 3.2QT

The Crux is the quietest air fryer we tested under $50. If you live in a small apartment or cook while others sleep, this matters more than you'd think. It's not the fastest, but the 3.2-quart capacity handles two servings comfortably.

Downsides: The exterior gets warm to the touch, and it takes about 30 seconds longer to preheat than the others.

Who should buy it: Apartment dwellers and anyone bothered by appliance noise. Crux 3.2QT

Gourmia 4QT

Gourmia packs the biggest basket you'll find under $50: a full 4 quarts. That's enough for a whole chicken or a big batch of wings. It also has a digital display and a few presets, which is rare at this price.

Downsides: Bigger basket means bigger footprint, and the presets are hit-or-miss.

Who should buy it: Families who cook in batches and want the most capacity for the money. Gourmia 4QT

Instant Vortex Mini 2QT

Instant made its name with pressure cookers, and their air fryers carry that reliability. The Vortex Mini is small, sleek, and cooks evenly. The interface is the most modern of the group, with a digital display and four one-touch presets.

Downsides: The 2-quart basket is the smallest here, and it's at the top of the budget at around $50.

Who should buy it: People who cook for one and want a dependable brand name. Instant Vortex Mini 2QT

NuWave Brio 3.5QT

The NuWave Brio stands out for its basket design — a wide, flat basket that crisps food evenly without constant shaking. It also comes with a rack insert for cooking two levels at once, which is a genuinely useful extra at this price.

Downsides: The rack is finicky to clean, and the unit is taller than most.

Who should buy it: Anyone who wants evenly crisped food and doesn't mind a taller appliance. NuWave Brio 3.5QT

The bottom line

For most people, the Chefman TurboFry 3.6QT is the best all-around air fryer under $50. If you cook for one, get the Cosori Mini. If you want the most capacity, the Gourmia 4QT is the value king. Whichever you choose, skip the fancy presets and focus on capacity, wattage, and a dishwasher-safe basket — those three things decide whether you'll actually use it.
