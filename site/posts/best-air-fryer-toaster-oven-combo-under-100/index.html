If you are tired of your toaster burning the bagels and your air fryer taking up half the counter, this article is for you. Combining both appliances into one machine saves space and money, but finding a good one under $100 can feel like a gamble. I have tested and compared the best budget-friendly combo units to help you figure out which one actually earns its spot on your countertop.

What to Look For

Before you hit "add to cart," keep these five criteria in mind. They separate the keepers from the return labels.

  • Wattage: Look for at least 1500 watts. Anything less and you will be waiting forever for the oven to preheat, and the air fry function will be weak.
  • Capacity: Check the interior dimensions. A 12-liter basket sounds big, but inside a toaster oven, the usable space is usually much smaller. Make sure a standard frozen pizza or four slices of bread actually fit.
  • Air Fry Basket Design: The best models come with a dedicated mesh basket that sits on top of the crumb tray. This allows for maximum airflow. Avoid models that just ask you to place food on the wire rack; that is just baking with a fan.
  • Controls: Dial knobs are simple and reliable but less precise. Digital touchscreens look great but can be finicky. I prefer a hybrid model with a dial for temperature and a digital timer for accuracy.
  • Heating Elements: Check if the top elements are exposed or hidden. Exposed elements are easier to clean but can burn food if you are not careful. Hidden elements (like in the Cuisinart models) are safer and easier to wipe down.

This is the model I recommend to anyone who just wants something that works without a manual. It has two simple mechanical dials—one for temperature and one for function/timer. The interior is deep enough for a 9-inch pie plate or a small casserole dish, which is rare at this price point.

Hamilton Beach Sure-Crisp Air Fryer Toaster Oven

Price: ~$95

This one is a beast. It is the largest model on this list, and it easily fits a 12-inch pizza or a whole chicken. If you cook for more than two people, this is the unit I would choose. It has a "Super Steam" function that adds moisture, but honestly, I rarely use it. The real star is the "Sure-Crisp" air fry basket that sits high in the oven cavity, close to the heating elements.

The basket is huge. I can fit a full pound of chicken wings in a single layer, which is impossible in most other budget combos. The controls are a mix of buttons and a dial. It takes a few minutes to understand the menu, but once you do, it is straightforward. It also has a "slow cook" setting, which doubles as a second crockpot in a pinch.

Downsides? It is heavy and takes up a lot of counter space. It also runs hot; I have to lower the temperature by 25 degrees compared to my regular oven. Cleanup is a bit tedious because the interior walls have a non-stick coating that scratches easily. Use only silicone or wooden tools inside. This is for the family cook who prioritizes volume over precise temperature control.

The capacity is smaller than the Hamilton Beach, so it is best for 2-3 people. If you want the most control over your cooking and do not mind a finicky screen, this is a great buy.

The Bottom Line

If you want the best balance of performance, size, and price, I would pick the Black+Decker Crisp 'N Bake. It is not the flashiest, but it is the most reliable and easiest to clean. If you have a bigger family, the Hamilton Beach Sure-Crisp is worth the extra few dollars for the capacity. And if you are a single person who wants something small and pretty, the Dash Deluxe is a solid choice.

All of these will do the job better than a traditional toaster oven. The key is to match the size and controls to your cooking style. Do not overthink it; pick the one that fits your counter space and your budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are air fryer toaster ovens worth it?

Yes, if you have limited counter space. They replace two appliances with one, which saves money and storage space. The air frying function is not as powerful as a dedicated basket-style air fryer, but it is good enough for most everyday meals like fries, chicken, and reheating leftovers.

Can I put aluminum foil in these ovens?

You can, but do not cover the crumb tray completely. Leaving the tray uncovered allows grease to drip down and burn, which creates smoke. I usually line just the cooking pan or basket with foil, leaving the tray exposed for airflow.

How do I clean the heating elements?

You should not clean the heating elements directly. Unplug the unit and wait for it to cool completely. Use a dry pastry brush or a vacuum with a brush attachment to remove loose crumbs. For baked-on grease, wipe the walls with a damp cloth, but never touch the elements themselves.
