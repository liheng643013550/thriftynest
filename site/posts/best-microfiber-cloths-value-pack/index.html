You don't need to spend a fortune to get a decent set of cleaning cloths. The truth is, for general dusting, wiping counters, and tackling glass, the cheapest microfiber cloths on the market often perform just as well as premium brands. This guide breaks down the best value packs so you can stock up without the sticker shock.

What to look for

Before you click "add to cart," keep these five criteria in mind. They separate a true bargain from a frustrating waste of money.

  • Weight and density: Look for a GSM (grams per square meter) between 200 and 300. Lower than 200 feels like thin tissue, while anything above 300 is overkill for everyday use.
  • Blend ratio: An 80/20 polyester-to-polyamide blend is the standard for effective cleaning. Too much polyester makes the cloth slick and useless for trapping dust.
  • Edge quality: Cheap cloths often have exposed, rough edges that scratch delicate surfaces. Look for stitched or laser-cut hems that lay flat.
  • Shedding test: Read recent reviews for mentions of lint or fibers left behind after wiping glass. A good cheap cloth should not shed.
  • Warranty or guarantee: The best cheap packs are cheap enough that you don't care if one tears, but a solid return policy still helps if the whole batch fails.

Amazon Basics Microfiber Cleaning Cloths

Price: Around $11 for a 24-pack.

This is the default recommendation for most people. These cloths are lightweight at around 220 GSM, which makes them perfect for quick wipes, dusting blinds, and cleaning bathroom fixtures. They do not have a thick, plush feel, but they grab dust well and hold up to repeated washing.

The downsides are minor. The edges are stitched but can fray after many hot washes. They also tend to be on the thinner side, so they are not ideal for soaking up large spills. For everyday surface cleaning, they are unbeatable for the price.

Who should buy it: Anyone who needs a no-fuss, bulk supply for general cleaning without worrying about ruining a "nice" cloth.

Scotch-Brite Large Microfiber Cloths

Price: Around $14 for a 12-pack.

Scotch-Brite makes a reliable, medium-weight cloth that feels a bit more substantial than the Amazon Basics option. These are slightly larger, which helps when you are wiping down big countertop sections or cleaning a full-length mirror. The weave is tight, so they pick up pet hair and fine dust without pushing it around.

The main downside is that they can feel a little stiff out of the package. A quick wash with fabric softener (or vinegar) softens them up nicely. They also are not the best for high-gloss surfaces like car paint, as the texture is a bit aggressive.

Who should buy it: Homeowners who want a recognizable brand name and a slightly thicker cloth for general daily cleaning.

Mr. Siga Microfiber Cleaning Cloths

Price: Around $17 for a 12-pack.

Mr. Siga is a bit of a hidden gem in the budget category. These cloths are noticeably thicker than the competition at this price point, with a softer, more plush pile. They excel at absorbing water, making them a good choice for drying dishes or wiping down wet bathroom tiles.

The color-coded edges are a practical touch, letting you separate kitchen cloths from bathroom cloths without guesswork. The downside is that the thick weave takes longer to dry, so you need to be diligent about airing them out to avoid a musty smell.

Who should buy it: People who want a thicker, more comfortable cloth without stepping up to premium pricing. Great for kitchens and bath areas.

Zwipes Microfiber Cleaning Cloths

Price: Around $20 for a 24-pack.

If you want the maximum number of cloths for your dollar, Zwipes is the winner. This pack gives you enough cloths to keep a set in every room, the garage, and the car. The quality is consistent with a standard 80/20 blend, and they handle grease and grime well.

The biggest flaw is the size. These run a bit smaller than standard cloths, so larger cleaning jobs require more folding and repositioning. They also are not as absorbent as the Mr. Siga cloths, so they are not the best for spill cleanup.

Who should buy it: Busy households that go through cloths quickly and want to avoid doing laundry every few days. Perfect for workshop or garage use.

HOMEXCEL Microfiber Cleaning Cloths

Price: Around $15 for a 12-pack.

HOMEXCEL has carved out a niche for budget-friendly detailing cloths. These are softer and fluffier than typical value packs, which makes them safer for use on screens, eyeglasses, and polished metal. They are also quite lint-resistant, so they leave a clean finish on glass and mirrors.

The trade-off is that the plush texture is not great for scrubbing dried-on messes. They tend to slide over sticky residue rather than scrape it off. Use them for final polishing and light dusting, not for heavy-duty scrubbing.

Who should buy it: Anyone who wants a dedicated set of soft cloths for electronics, stainless steel, and streak-free glass without paying for premium microfiber.

E-Cloth General Purpose Cleaning Cloths

Price: Around $22 for a 10-pack.

E-Cloth is a step up in quality, and it shows. These cloths use a finer fiber that creates a high-static charge, which pulls dust and dirt into the cloth rather than just pushing it around. They are excellent for households with allergies or pets, as they trap more particles per swipe.

The downside is the price per cloth is higher than the others on this list. They also require a bit more care in the wash, as fabric softener will ruin their cleaning power. If you follow the care instructions, they last for years.

Who should buy it: Budget-conscious buyers who are willing to pay a little more upfront for a cloth that lasts longer and cleans more effectively on the first pass.

The bottom line

Cheap microfiber cloths are fine for 90 percent of cleaning tasks. For most people, the Amazon Basics or Mr. Siga packs offer the best balance of price, thickness, and durability. If you need maximum quantity for a workshop or rental property, go with Zwipes. If you want a softer cloth for glass and electronics, HOMEXCEL is the smart pick.

Do not overthink this purchase. Buy a bulk pack, wash them before first use, and replace them when they start to smell or lose their grip. You will save money and keep your home clean without ever needing a $10 single cloth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What GSM range should a budget microfiber cloth fall into?

Aim for a weight between 200 and 300 GSM. Anything under 200 tends to feel flimsy, while cloths above 300 are heavier than most everyday cleaning jobs require. The Amazon Basics cloths sit at roughly 220 GSM, which fits comfortably in that window.

Which pack is the cheapest per cloth?

The Amazon Basics set runs about $11 for 24 cloths, which works out to the lowest cost per cloth of any option listed. Zwipes is the next closest on volume, offering 24 cloths for around $20. If quantity matters more than a plush feel, either of these stretches a dollar furthest.

Which cloths work best on glass and electronics?

The HOMEXCEL 12-pack is the pick for streak-free glass, screens, eyeglasses, and polished metal, since its softer, fluffier pile resists lint. The E-Cloth General Purpose Pack is another strong choice for glass and for homes with pets or allergy concerns, thanks to a finer fiber that holds onto particles. Just keep fabric softener away from the E-Cloth, as it damages the cleaning performance.
