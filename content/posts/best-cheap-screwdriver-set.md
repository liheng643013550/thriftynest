You don't need to spend $40 on a screwdriver set to fix a loose cabinet hinge or open up a laptop. But you do need one that won't strip screws or fall apart on the second use. I've bought plenty of cheap sets over the years, and the difference between a good $12 set and a bad $12 set comes down to a few small details.

Honestly, Magnetic tips are at the top of that list. A magnetized tip holds the screw so you can start it one-handed in a tight spot instead of chasing it across the floor. Here's what actually matters when you're shopping on a budget, plus the sets I'd recommend to a friend.

## What to look for in a cheap screwdriver set

- Magnetic tips. This is non-negotiable for me. Without magnetization, small screws drop constantly, and you'll spend more time hunting for them than working. Most sets under $20 include it, but check the listing.
- Tip variety. A set with only Phillips and flathead will leave you stuck on Torx, hex, or square-drive screws. Look for at least 6 to 10 tip types if you want one kit to cover furniture, electronics, and appliances.
- Steel quality. Chrome vanadium (Cr-V) or S2 steel holds up far better than no-name mystery metal. Cheap steel rounds off at the tip, which is exactly what strips screws.
- Handle comfort. You'll grip a handle harder than you think when a screw is stuck. Rubberized or textured grips beat smooth plastic, especially during a 20-minute furniture assembly session.
- Storage case. A labeled case keeps bits from scattering in a junk drawer. It sounds minor until you've lost the one Torx bit you need.
- Bit holder vs. fixed drivers. Interchangeable bit sets cover more ground for less money. Fixed-handle drivers feel sturdier but cost more per tool.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are cheap screwdriver sets worth buying?

Yes, for occasional household use. A $12 to $20 set handles furniture, cabinets, and basic repairs fine. They wear out faster than pro-grade tools, but most people never put in enough hours to notice.

### Do magnetic screwdriver tips really matter?

They do. A magnetic tip holds the screw in place so you can start it one-handed, which matters in tight spots like inside a cabinet or behind a desk. It also stops screws from dropping into places you can't reach, in my experience.

### What's the difference between Phillips and Pozidriv screws?

They look similar, but Pozidriv screws have extra marks between the slots and need a Pozidriv driver for a proper fit. Using a Phillips driver on a Pozidriv screw can strip the head, so check your set's bit list if you work on European furniture or appliances.
