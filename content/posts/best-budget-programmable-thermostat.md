## What to look for

That said, Before you buy, keep these five things in mind. They matter more than fancy features.

- **Compatibility:** Check if your system is 24V (standard gas/electric) or 120V/240V (baseboard heat). Most budget smart thermostats only work with 24V systems. Look for a compatibility checker on the brand’s website before you order.
- **Battery vs. C-Wire:** Some models run on AA batteries, which makes installation easier if you do not have a common wire. Others need a C-wire for constant power. If you rent, grab a battery-powered unit to avoid wiring hassles.
- **App Quality:** A cheap thermostat with a buggy app is a headache. Look for a model with at least a 4-star app rating on the Apple App Store or Google Play. You will use the app more than the physical dial.
- **Schedule Flexibility:** The best budget thermostats let you set different temperatures for weekdays and weekends. If you work shift work, make sure the unit supports a 5-2 or 5-1-1 schedule, not just a single 7-day loop.
- **Energy Reporting:** Not essential, but nice. Some apps show you a monthly report of how long your system ran. I find this helps me actually stick to my savings goals.
- **Physical Controls:** Do not ignore the hardware. A touchscreen that is laggy or a dial that feels cheap will annoy you daily. Read recent reviews for complaints about button response.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will a programmable thermostat really save me money?

Yes, if you use it correctly. The U.S. Department of Energy says you can save about 10% a year on heating and cooling by setting your thermostat back 7-10 degrees Fahrenheit for 8 hours a day. A programmable thermostat automates this so you do not forget.

### Do I need a C-wire for a smart thermostat?

Most smart thermostats need a C-wire for power. However, some models like the Emerson Sensi Touch and the Honeywell T3 can run on batteries or come with a power adapter kit. Check your existing thermostat wires first. If you see a blue or black wire connected to a terminal labeled "C", you are good to go, believe it or not.

### What is the difference between a smart thermostat and a programmable thermostat?

A programmable thermostat lets you set a fixed schedule, like 68°F at 7 AM and 62°F at 10 PM. A smart thermostat connects to Wi-Fi, so you can adjust it from your phone, and it often has features like geofencing or motion detection that adjust the temperature automatically based on your presence.
