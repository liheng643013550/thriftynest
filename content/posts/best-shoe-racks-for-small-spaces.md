## What to look for

Before you buy, keep these things in mind. They will save you from buying something you will hate in a month.

- **Measure your floor space.** Know the width and depth of your entryway, closet, or behind-the-door spot. A rack that is 30 inches wide will not fit in a 24-inch nook.
- **Count your shoes.** Do you need space for 4 pairs or 12? Overlapping shoes on a tiered rack can be annoying, so buy for your actual daily rotation, not your whole collection.
- **Check the material.** Bamboo looks nice but can warp in humid climates. Metal and plastic are more durable and easier to clean. Particleboard is cheap but will not survive water puddles.
- **Look for open sides.** Solid sides trap dirt and make shoes harder to slide in. Open wire or slatted designs let air flow and keep things visible.
- **Think about assembly.** Many cheap racks require a screwdriver and patience. If you hate tools, look for a pop-up or foldable option that takes no time at all.
