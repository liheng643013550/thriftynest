Sitting all day is a silent budget killer—not just for your health, but for your focus and energy. A standing desk converter lets you switch between sitting and standing without replacing your entire desk, but the good ones often cost as much as a new piece of furniture. I’ve tested and researched the most popular models under $100 so you can stand up for your posture without sitting down on your wallet.

## What to look for

Worth noting, Before you buy, keep these five things in mind. They matter more than brand names or fancy gas springs.

- **Weight capacity:** Check what your monitor setup weighs. Many budget converters max out around 35 pounds, which is fine for one monitor and a laptop, but tight for dual 27-inch displays plus a keyboard.
- **Keyboard tray depth:** Your arms should rest at a 90-degree angle when typing. If the tray is too shallow, your wrists will bend and you’ll feel it by day three. Look for at least 10 inches of depth.
- **Height range:** Most converters lift from about 3 inches up to 16 or 17 inches. If you’re over 5’10” or your desk is unusually low, double-check the top height. My own desk is 29 inches tall, and I need the full 16-inch lift to get my elbows at a right angle.
- **Assembly time:** Some models arrive fully assembled. Others need a screwdriver and 20 minutes of your patience. If you hate tools, spend a few extra dollars on a pre-built option.
- **Stability at max height:** Read the reviews for wobble complaints. A converter that shakes at full extension is useless for writing or video calls. I’ve knocked over a coffee mug on a wobbly unit before—learn from my mistake.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will a standing desk converter fit on any desk?

Most converters work on desks that are 24 to 30 inches deep and at least 40 inches wide. They don’t require any drilling, so they’re safe for rental furniture. Just make sure your desk surface is flat and not glass—the rubber feet need a solid grip, in practice.

### How much weight can a budget converter hold?

Expect 20 to 35 pounds in the under-$100 range. A single monitor with a laptop is fine on any model. For dual monitors, check the weight of each screen and add them together before buying.

### Do I need to assemble the converter myself?

It depends on the brand. The VIVO and MOUNTUP models arrive mostly pre-assembled and take under five minutes to set up. The Ergotron and Mount-It! require 15 to 20 minutes with a screwdriver or hex key. None of them need power tools.
